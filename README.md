### Things I've built and shipped

**[ProperPDF](https://tryproperpdf.app)** is the one I'm proudest of. I wanted a PDF app that didn't make me upload my documents anywhere, so I built one for Android and iOS. You can merge, split, compress, sign and fill forms, and even chat with your PDF, all offline.
[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.github.thegbguy.pdfmate) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/properpdf-pdf-editor-offline/id6760956540)

**[Nepali Unicode Keyboard](https://github.com/theGBguy/nepali-unicode-keyboard)** lets you type Nepali in any app. Type `namaste` and get नमस्ते, or switch to the traditional layout, handwriting, or voice on Android. Nothing you type leaves your phone, and the whole thing is open source.
[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.github.thegbguy.nepalikeyboard) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nepali-unicode-keyboard/id6831912584)

**[ProperImage](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/properimage-heic-to-jpg-pdf/id6778331646)** started because I got tired of HEIC photos that wouldn't open anywhere. It converts, resizes, compresses and removes backgrounds on your iPhone or iPad without sending a single photo to a server.

**[KML KMZ Viewer](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kml-kmz-viewer-open-map-files/id6818108327)** lets you open map files on your iPhone and actually explore them: stack layers on Apple Maps, search through every place and attribute, and measure lines and areas. It was a really fun one to build.

**[GPS Simulator](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.github.thegbguy.gpssimulator)** is the tool I wish I'd had back at Dimitra. In the early days, I sometimes walked farm boundaries myself to collect coordinates for farm registration, until I added KML import to Connected Farmer. It's for Android developers and anyone else who needs their phone somewhere it isn't: drop a pin, draw a route on the map or import a GPX or KML file, and your phone follows along through Android's mock location setting.

**[Mero Contacts](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.github.thegbguy.merocontacts)** (मेरो सम्पर्क) is a contacts and calling app made for elderly Nepali users. It's Nepali first, with big text, favourites as large photos, names read out loud, and an optional "are you sure?" before every call.

Before going independent, I had a great time working with some amazing teams: [Entervu](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.entervu.entervu), [Bracketit](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.spiralogics.bracketit) and [Nepal Sambat](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.spiralogics.nepalsambat) at Spiralogics, and [Connected Farmer](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mobile.dimitra) at Dimitra. I learned a ton from every one of them.

### Open source

- [**Nepali Unicode Keyboard**](https://github.com/theGBguy/nepali-unicode-keyboard): typing Nepali on a phone should be easy, so I built a keyboard for Android and iOS where `namaste` turns into नमस्ते, and kept all of it open so anyone can check that nothing they type leaves the phone
- [**USSDNavigator**](https://github.com/theGBguy/USSDNavigator): buying a data pack through USSD menus was a pain, so I made Android do the tapping for me ❤️
- [**BarabiseyApp**](https://github.com/theGBguy/BarabiseyApp): a Compose Multiplatform app that keeps the people of Barabise up to date with local events and government notices
- [**KomposeNepaliDatePicker**](https://github.com/theGBguy/KomposeNepaliDatePicker): Nepal runs on Bikram Sambat, so I ported a Nepali date picker to Compose Multiplatform
- [**CMPFTSRoom**](https://github.com/theGBguy/CMPFTSRoom): a small sample showing full-text search with Room on Android and iOS
- [**MoviesCMP**](https://github.com/theGBguy/MoviesCMP): a TMDB movie browser I built while learning Compose Multiplatform
